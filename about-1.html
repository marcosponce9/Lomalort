About Us

We are an independent studio that develops its own games, but we also offer services to collaborate on other projects or create custom games. We can work with other studios to bring their games to life or design unique experiences for events like weddings and birthdays.

The Story

The idea of making video games began in October 2019, when I started learning on my own how to develop games while living in Tucumán. I had the goal of returning to Mendoza to form a team with friends and create our own studio. However, not finding people to work with, I decided to go solo and move forward with development.

In 2021, I founded LomaLort, and the following year we launched our first game, Spiriat. This project was a great learning experience, as it allowed me to explore all stages of game development, from the initial concept to launch. It was a key experience that gave me the confidence to revisit a project I had paused due to lack of experience: Marcux: Medieval Alien Invasion.

Today, at LomaLort, we continue to work with that initial vision of creating our own worlds and experiences. We want to share our stories and connect with players who enjoy a unique universe, crafted with passion and dedication.
